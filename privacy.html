Free Mahjong Solitaire ("we," "us," or "the site") is a free browser game at easymahjongsolitaire.com. This page explains what data the site itself stores and how third-party advertising and analytics services may use data when you visit.

Game data stays on your device

Free Mahjong Solitaire has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server that stores your information. Your current board, move history, settings (tile size, tile style, sound, timer visibility), play statistics (games played, games won, best time), achievement badges, and Daily Challenge completion history are saved only in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on your own device. None of this is transmitted to us or to any server — it's simply how the "continue where you left off" feature, your badges, and your preferences persist between visits. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a private/incognito window, removes it.

Settings includes an optional Backup & Restore tool that turns your stats, badges, and Daily Challenge history into a short text code you can copy and paste back in yourself (for example, on another device). Creating or restoring a code never sends anything to us — it's a local export/import between your own devices, and what you do with that code afterward (saving it, sharing it) is entirely up to you.

Advertising

We intend to show advertising on this site through Google AdSense and similar third-party ad networks to help cover hosting costs. When active, these partners may use cookies, web beacons, or advertising identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. We don't control these technologies directly — they are operated by the ad providers themselves.

You can see and adjust the personalized ad settings Google has for you at Google Ads Settings, and you can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Depending on where you live, you may also be able to opt out of personalized advertising generally through the Digital Advertising Alliance or your browser and device settings.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to see basic, aggregated information about site traffic (such as page views and which pages are popular) so we can tell whether the site is working well. It does not use cookies and does not track you individually across other websites.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since there is no account system and no server-side data collection by us. Third-party advertising, where active, is subject to the ad provider's own policies and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
